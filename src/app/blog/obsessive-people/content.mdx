# I love obsessive people

I always find myself drawn to people who are obsessive over anything. I just listen and I get the most joy out of learning about that makes people happy. I’m not talking about the side projects, I’m talking about the people who live for things. They are extremely knowledgeable about it and are so passionate about it that all they want to share it with others.

I’ve spent 2 hours in antique shop that was all themed Coca-Cola products talking to the owner about where he’s collected everything. I learned that there was a presidents edition bottle, how the bottles changes formula in the 1900s. I have a friend’s dad who’s obsessed with hot wheels cars, every wall in his garage is filled with cars from floor to ceiling. There's another guy who I see once a year at a fundraising event and I spend hours every year talking to him about plants, he knows each Latin name of the plant, how long he’s raised them, and how to properly take care of them.

I'm really not all to interested about these subjects in particular, but hearing about something that makes somebody ticks makes it infinitely more interesting to me. And I just sit there and listen, ask leading questions to learn more and make a friend in the process. Hearing people share what they are passionate in is one of my biggest joys in life.
